# WonderLoop Privacy Notice — Starter

WonderLoop should collect only the data required to operate the service. Before public launch, replace this starter with a jurisdiction-reviewed privacy policy that explains account data, generated content, analytics, storage duration, deletion, cookies, third-party AI providers, international transfers, and contact information.

Do not collect children's personal information through the creator workflow. Prompts and generated content should be treated as user content and moderated according to the final service policy.
